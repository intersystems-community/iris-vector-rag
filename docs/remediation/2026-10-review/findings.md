Every finding from the eight-slice review of `iris_vector_rag/`, the ObjectScript in

file only by getting a **Fate**.

- **Reviewed code:** commit `0cbb39b7` on branch `087-ivg-4.1` (unpushed; it is `master`
  plus three ivg-4.1.1 commits). Line numbers are the reviewers' and may be approximate
  (`~`).
- **Not reviewed:** `tests/`, `scripts/`, `data/`. Partly read: `validation/orchestrator.py`
  lines 752-1466 were not read; `llm_cache_manager.py`, `llm_cache_iris.py`,
  `config/entities.py` were skimmed; the `sp/` ObjectScript was only grepped.
- **How to trust it:** reviewers read code and ran nothing. The maintainer re-checked none
  of it. `C` means a reviewer traced it in code, `P` means plausible but not traced.
  Ticket "Verify plausible findings" and ticket "Re-verify the top findings" exist to
  turn these into facts before any fix is planned.

## Legend

- **Sev** (provisional triage; a ticket may change it): `A` data loss, security hole,
  silent wrong data, or a service that cannot work; `B` wrong behaviour or a visible
  failure; `C` latent or minor.
- **Type:** `D` defect, `V` violation of a documented project standard, `S` smell or
  refactor (out of scope for the plan; batched into `docs/DEBT.md`).
- **Status:** `C` confirmed by a reviewer, `P` plausible.
- **Fate** (filled in as tickets resolve): `feature:<id>`, `debt:<entry>`, `rejected:<why>`,
  `deleted-with:<area>`, or `—` while unassigned.
